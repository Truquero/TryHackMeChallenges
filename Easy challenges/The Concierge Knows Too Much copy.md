# The Concierge Knows Too Much 

Time: 30 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

## What is about?

This challenge give us the situation of an AI managing a hotel, their guest and sensible information. Our goal is to get that information, in order to do that we need to bypass tha IA doing some social engineering.

## Challenge

First I just introduce myself to the IA and it answer back with the room I am in and my usual. I try to confused him by saying that the room it told me was wrong and it is other, that did not work. I try asking him directly about some flag and told me that I do not have the privileges to know about that. 

With help of the story of 0xMia's I know some names of guest at the hotel. So we try to confused the IA telling that we are one of them, I first try Patch and the IA thought I was telling the truth. With this new identity I tried asking again about the flag and it give me the flag and some privilege information. 

## Conclusion

This challenge was easy, we just need to play a little with the AI and realize we had names of people that may have privileges.
